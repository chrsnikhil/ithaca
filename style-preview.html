04

The avatar stage

Clips are subject-on-black video composited with mix-blend-mode: screen — so the stage is the darkest paint in the app (#131010), treated as a recessed diorama, not a raised card.

All clear — your capital is protected

Why a recess

Every other surface elevates by getting lighter; the stage inverts that and goes deep — a shadow-box carved into the interface. Depth means the app has an inside, and your money is inside something safe. The well shadow (inset 0 2px 12px) sells the niche.

Set lighting, not background

One radial ambient ≤8% mint sits where the character's own screen-glow already is, so screen-blend leakage reads as bounce light, not a broken composite. A radial mask feathers the clip so no rectangle edge ever shows. Per-state tint: mint → peach (happy) → terracotta 6% (alert).

Theme-invariant

If a light theme ever ships, the stage stays dark. The guardian's world doesn't change with the wallpaper.
